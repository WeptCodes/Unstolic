Practical engine care

Use your owner's manual for vehicle-specific guidance and service intervals.

Check your oil

Check oil at the intervals in your owner's manual, and use the recommended oil grade and level.

Watch the temperature

If the temperature rises or a warning light appears, pull over safely and arrange an inspection.

Choose suitable fuel

Use good-quality fuel that meets your vehicle manufacturer's recommendations.

Notice unusual sounds

Have new or unusual engine noises checked promptly by a qualified technician.

Service on schedule

Follow the manufacturer's service schedule to identify maintenance needs early.

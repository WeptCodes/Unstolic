Privacy Policy

At Unstolic Resources Limited, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

1. Who we are

Unstolic Resources Limited is a registered company in Zambia operating as a Kwik-Fit franchisee. We provide automotive care, maintenance, fleet servicing, supply and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect and share personal information when you interact with us, including through our website, phone, email, in-person visits, or other business contacts.

2. Information we may collect

We may collect personal information when you:

  • Contact us through our website, including the contact form, phone, email or social media channels
  • Request a service, quote, booking, or support enquiry
  • Visit our premises or speak with our staff
  • Provide information as part of a vehicle servicing, fleet management or business relationship
  • Use cookies or analytics tools on our website

This may include your name, address, phone number, email address, company name, vehicle details, service history, payment details, and any other information necessary to provide services or respond to requests.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide automotive care, maintenance, supply and fleet support services
  • Respond to enquiries, bookings, quotations and support requests
  • Manage customer and fleet records
  • Process payments and maintain financial records
  • Improve our service quality, operations and website experience
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and tax requirements
  • Protect against fraud, misuse and security threats

4. Legal basis for processing

We process personal information on the basis that it is necessary for:

  • Contract performance and the provision of goods or services
  • Our legitimate business interests, where those interests are balanced against your rights
  • Legal and regulatory compliance
  • Your consent where required, such as for marketing or optional communications

5. Cookies and website analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve usability, understand visitor behaviour, and support digital analytics. Cookies can help us remember preferences, measure site performance and understand which pages are useful to visitors.

If you opt in to analytics cookies, we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the website, including through heatmaps and session recordings. Clarity is loaded only after you consent to analytics cookies.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

6. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Our staff and authorised service providers, where this is necessary to deliver our services
  • Website hosting and form-processing providers, including Netlify, to receive and manage contact form submissions
  • Microsoft Clarity, our website analytics provider, if you consent to analytics cookies
  • Financial institutions or payment processors for billing and payments
  • Professional advisers, auditors and legal representatives
  • Authorities or regulators where required by law
  • Third parties where required to protect our rights, property or safety, or that of others

Where we engage third-party service providers, we require them to handle information in a secure and lawful manner.

7. International transfer

Some information may be processed outside Zambia when necessary for operational or technical support. We take reasonable steps to ensure that such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards and handled in compliance with applicable privacy laws.

8. Data security

We take reasonable precautions to protect personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure or alteration. These measures include restricted access, secure systems, and operational controls. However, no internet-based service can be completely risk-free, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law, contractual obligations, accounting or regulatory requirements.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational requirements
  • Object to or restrict processing of your information
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis for processing
  • Request information about the source of personal data where it was not collected directly from you

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

11. Children’s privacy

Our services are generally intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate safeguards or parental consent where required by law.

12. Third-party links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. Please review the privacy notices of any third-party sites you visit.

13. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements or business operations. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
